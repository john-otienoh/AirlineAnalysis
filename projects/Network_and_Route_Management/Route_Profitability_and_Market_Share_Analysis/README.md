### 03 · Route Profitability and Market Share Analysis

**Goal:** Assess the financial performance of each route and determine the airline's competitive positioning. Identify high-performing routes, opportunities for improvement, and potential new market entries.

**Data Required:**
- Revenue and cost data per route (fuel, labor, landing fees)
- Load factor and yield data for each route
- Competitor market share data and route frequencies
- Average fare pricing and competitive pricing trends
- Passenger demographics and demand patterns
- Seasonal demand fluctuations
- Airport charges and operational costs per route
- Regulatory restrictions on pricing or capacity

**Key Formulas:**
```
Route Profitability = (Route Revenue - Route Cost) / Route Revenue × 100
Load Factor        = (RPK / ASK) × 100
Yield              = Total Passenger Revenue / RPK
Market Share       = (Airline Passengers on Route / Total Market Passengers) × 100
```

**Methodology:**
1. Calculate Route Profitability
2. Analyze Load Factor and Yield
3. Examine Seasonal Demand Patterns
4. Measure Market Share per Route
5. Competitor Analysis (frequency, pricing, market share)
6. Pricing and Yield Strategy Review
7. Cost Structure Evaluation (fuel, crew, airport fees)
8. Scenario Analysis for Potential Adjustments

**Output Format:**
- Route Profitability Table
- Market Share Summary (vs. competitors)
- Seasonal Demand Chart
- Scenario Analysis Results

**Improvement Steps:**
1. Adjust capacity on low-demand routes
2. Implement targeted dynamic pricing
3. Increase presence in high-growth-potential markets
4. Pursue cost reduction initiatives on high-cost routes
5. Optimize aircraft allocation for demand alignment
