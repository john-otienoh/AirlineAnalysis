### 06 · Competitor Route Overlap and Market Penetration Analysis

**Goal:** Understand the level of competition on existing routes and assess the airline's market share relative to competitors. Identify where competitors are gaining traction or where market gaps offer opportunities.

**Data Required:**
- Competitor flight schedules, frequencies, and aircraft types on overlapping routes
- Market share data by route (passenger volumes per airline)
- Average fare prices and pricing trends across competing routes
- Load factors and yield data
- Seasonal demand variations and peak travel periods
- Customer demographics and loyalty data by route
- Historical data on competitor entry or exit from routes

**Key Formulas:**
```
Market Share = (Airline Passengers on Route / Total Market Passengers) × 100
Load Factor  = (RPK / ASK) × 100
Yield        = Total Revenue / RPK
```

**Methodology:**
1. Identify Competing Routes (frequency, aircraft, competitor presence)
2. Calculate Market Share on Overlapping Routes (track trends over time)
3. Analyze Load Factors and Yield vs. Competitors
4. Evaluate Competitor Pricing and Fare Trends
5. Assess Seasonal Demand Impact
6. Analyze Route Frequency and Capacity Differences
7. Review Competitor Route Entry/Exit Trends
8. Scenario Testing for Strategic Adjustments

**Output Format:**
- Market Share Report (by route vs. key competitors)
- Load Factor and Yield Comparison
- Competitor Pricing Summary
- Scenario Analysis Results

**Improvement Steps:**
1. Enhance competitive pricing on high-competition routes
2. Strengthen loyalty programs and targeted promotions
3. Optimize capacity and frequency to match competitors
4. Differentiate in-flight services for premium segments
5. Monitor competitor movements proactively
