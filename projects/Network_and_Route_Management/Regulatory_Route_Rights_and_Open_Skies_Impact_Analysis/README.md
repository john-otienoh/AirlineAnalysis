### 07 · Regulatory Route Rights and Open Skies Impact Analysis

**Goal:** Evaluate the regulatory landscape affecting route rights and international market access. Identify constraints and opportunities from Open Skies agreements, bilateral air service agreements, and route restrictions.

**Data Required:**
- Bilateral air service agreements between countries
- Open Skies agreements and terms for relevant markets
- Regulatory restrictions on frequency, capacity, and pricing
- Historical route approval/denial data
- Competitor access and frequencies on regulated routes
- Traffic rights (e.g., Fifth Freedom rights) for each market
- Passenger demand and load factors on impacted routes
- Government-imposed restrictions or slot controls

**Methodology:**
1. Review Relevant Air Service Agreements (frequency caps, aircraft type restrictions, slot allocations)
2. Assess Open Skies Provisions (unlimited capacity and frequency rights)
3. Identify Traffic Rights (including Fifth Freedom rights)
4. Evaluate Competitor Access and Regulatory Compliance
5. Analyze Impact of Route Restrictions on profitability and competitiveness
6. Assess Slot Availability and Airport Constraints
7. Scenario Testing for Regulatory Adjustments (expanded Open Skies, new route rights)

**Output Format:**
- Regulatory Compliance Summary (by route: frequency, capacity, slot limits)
- Traffic Rights and Market Access Overview
- Competitor Compliance Report
- Scenario Analysis Results

**Improvement Steps:**
1. Leverage traffic rights (e.g., Fifth Freedom) for market expansion
2. Engage in lobbying to negotiate additional route rights or slots
3. Optimize slot utilization in less congested time windows
4. Align aircraft types and frequencies to comply while maximizing load
5. Monitor Open Skies and bilateral agreement updates in real-time
