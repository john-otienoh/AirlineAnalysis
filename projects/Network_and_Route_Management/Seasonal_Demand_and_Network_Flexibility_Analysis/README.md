### 04 · Seasonal Demand and Network Flexibility Analysis

**Goal:** Understand passenger demand fluctuations across seasons and evaluate the airline's ability to adjust its network accordingly. Optimize route schedules, capacity allocation, and operational efficiency to maximize revenue and minimize costs across peak and off-peak periods.

**Data Required:**
- Historical passenger load data by route and season (monthly/quarterly)
- Load factors and revenue per route by season
- Regional holiday and event calendars affecting travel demand
- Aircraft availability and utilization data
- Competitor seasonal schedules and frequency adjustments
- Cost data for operational adjustments (crew, fuel)
- Route-level profitability metrics per season

**Key Formula:**
```
Seasonal Load Factor = (RPK in Season / ASK in Season) × 100
```

**Methodology:**
1. Analyze Historical Demand Patterns (identify peak and off-peak periods)
2. Calculate Seasonal Load Factors per route
3. Determine Seasonal Revenue Performance
4. Evaluate Network Flexibility (speed of adaptation)
5. Analyze Competitor Seasonal Adjustments
6. Capacity Allocation and Aircraft Type Adjustment
7. Seasonal Pricing Strategy Review
8. Scenario Analysis for Flexibility Improvements

**Output Format:**
- Seasonal Load Factor Table
- Seasonal Revenue Performance Summary
- Competitor Seasonal Adjustment Overview
- Scenario Analysis Results

**Improvement Steps:**
1. Optimize capacity in off-peak periods (smaller aircraft, reduced frequency)
2. Increase capacity during peak seasons
3. Adjust pricing seasonally (dynamic pricing)
4. Introduce seasonal-only routes to high-demand destinations
5. Enhance operational flexibility for rapid scheduling and crew adjustments
