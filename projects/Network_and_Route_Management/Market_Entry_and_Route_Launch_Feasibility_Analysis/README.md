### 05 · Market Entry and Route Launch Feasibility Analysis

**Goal:** Evaluate the potential success of entering a new market or launching a new route. Determine if demand, competitive landscape, and financial outlook justify the investment.

**Data Required:**
- Passenger demand forecasts for the target route or market
- Demographic and economic data for the target region
- Competitor presence (frequency, market share, pricing)
- Regulatory environment and route right restrictions
- Operating cost estimates (fuel, crew, airport fees, maintenance)
- Aircraft availability and suitable fleet options
- Historical data on comparable routes for benchmarking

**Key Formula:**
```
Projected Profitability = (Projected Revenue - Projected Cost) / Projected Revenue × 100
```

**Methodology:**
1. Conduct Market Demand Analysis
2. Evaluate Competitor Presence and Market Share
3. Assess Regulatory Feasibility (Open Skies, bilateral agreements)
4. Estimate Route Profitability (with all operational costs)
5. Determine Aircraft Requirements and Fleet Availability
6. Operational and Infrastructure Evaluation
7. Develop Preliminary Pricing Strategy
8. Scenario Testing and Risk Analysis (optimistic / base / conservative)

**Output Format:**
- Demand Forecast Report
- Competitor Analysis Summary
- Regulatory Compliance Checklist
- Profitability Projection Table
- Risk Analysis Summary (matrix of risks and impact estimates)

**Improvement Steps:**
1. Implement targeted marketing pre-launch
2. Negotiate favorable airport and landing fees
3. Leverage code-sharing or alliances for market reach
4. Adjust initial capacity (start small, scale with demand)
5. Monitor competitor and regulatory developments continuously
