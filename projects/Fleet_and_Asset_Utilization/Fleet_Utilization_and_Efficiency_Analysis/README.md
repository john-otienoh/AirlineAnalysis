### 08 · Fleet Utilization and Efficiency Analysis

**Goal:** Optimize the use of the airline's aircraft fleet by analyzing utilization rates, fuel efficiency, and operational performance. Improve asset efficiency, reduce operational costs, and ensure fleet deployment aligns with route demand and profitability goals.

**Data Required:**
- Flight hours and cycles (takeoff/landing) per aircraft type
- Fuel consumption and efficiency metrics per aircraft type and route
- Maintenance schedules and downtime per aircraft
- Operational costs per aircraft (crew, fuel, maintenance)
- Route demand data and load factors
- Aircraft lease or purchase costs and lease expiry dates
- Industry utilization benchmarks

**Key Formulas:**
```
Utilization Rate       = Total Flight Hours / Total Days in Operation
Fuel Efficiency        = Total Fuel Consumed / Total Passenger Kilometers
Downtime Percentage    = (Total Days Out of Service / Total Days in Operation) × 100
CASK                   = Total Operating Costs / Available Seat Kilometers (ASK)
```

**Methodology:**
1. Calculate Aircraft Utilization Rates (by type, identify underutilized assets)
2. Analyze Fuel Efficiency by Aircraft Type (per RPK and ASK)
3. Evaluate Maintenance Downtime (flag high-downtime aircraft)
4. Match Aircraft Types to Route Demand
5. Assess Cost Per Available Seat Kilometer (CASK)
6. Benchmark Utilization Against Industry Standards
7. Scenario Testing for Fleet Reallocation

**Output Format:**
- Utilization Rate Summary Table
- Fuel Efficiency Report
- Maintenance Downtime Report
- CASK Comparison Table
- Scenario Analysis Results

**Improvement Steps:**
1. Redeploy underutilized aircraft to higher-demand routes
2. Reassign fuel-inefficient aircraft to shorter routes
3. Optimize maintenance schedules to minimize downtime
4. Match fleet assignments to route demand profiles
5. Eliminate or modify routes with consistently high CASK values
